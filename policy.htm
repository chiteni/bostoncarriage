Waiting time

Applies only to point-to-point service. Waiting time charges are assessed hourly. We must have the correct airline and flight number for all airport pickups.

Domestic airport arrivals
Waiting charges begin 15 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. After 15 minutes, waiting time is retroactive to the scheduled arrival time. We do not charge waiting time for flight delays.
International airport arrivals
Waiting charges begin 45 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. After 45 minutes, waiting time is retroactive to the scheduled arrival time. We do not charge waiting time for flight delays.
Non-airport pickups
Waiting charges begin 15 minutes after the scheduled pickup time. After 15 minutes, waiting time is retroactive to the scheduled pickup time.
Trains
Train schedules cannot be monitored, so billing begins at the scheduled pickup time.

Cancellations

The date on which Boston Carriage or its agents receive your cancellation notice determines the applicable fee. We recommend following up with a phone call. We do not take responsibility for cancellation fees resulting from mistakes, weather, flight cancellations, family emergencies, double bookings, or wrong dates or information.

Free cancellation window: a reservation can be cancelled at no cost only if the request is made within 1 hour after the reservation was made. After that, the deposit is not refunded.

Point-to-point service

Round trips must be cancelled as two separate reservations. A nonrefundable 30% deposit is required. A cancellation fee equal to 30% of the trip cost applies to any cancellation from the time of booking up to 24 hours before pickup. Cancelling within 24 hours of pickup results in a full charge for the trip.

Hourly service

Applies to all vehicles. A nonrefundable 30% deposit is charged at booking. Cancellations within 7 days of the scheduled pickup forfeit 100% of the total bill.

Wedding and prom packages

Applies to all vehicles. All wedding and prom packages are 100% non-refundable from the date and time of booking, whether or not the service is used. Packages are billed garage to garage: the hour starts when the vehicle leaves our Boston facility and ends when it returns.

Chargebacks from credit card issuing banks are subject to all of the above policies.

Changes to a booking

Any change to an existing reservation must be made by calling 617-314-6538. We will email you a revised confirmation with the updated details.

If you change the drop-off location during a ride, additional charges apply based on the new location. For hourly service, extra hours can be added at the original rate as long as the vehicle is available. If it isn't, service ends at the time in the original agreement.

During the ride

  • All vehicles are non-smoking. Alcohol is allowed only in limousines, and eating is not recommended.
  • Passengers may not open doors while the vehicle is moving, throw objects from the vehicle, shout, or put any part of their body outside it.
  • Stay seated at all times, even when the vehicle is stopped in traffic. Underage passengers must be accompanied by one or two adults.
  • Please let the chauffeur open and close all doors, for everyone's safety. Clients and their guests agree to maintain acceptable personal conduct at all times.
  • If a passenger gets sick in the vehicle or damages the interior, a $300 cleaning fee is charged to the card on file.
  • The customer accepts a minimum responsibility of $300 for physical damage caused by any passenger, including improper use of audio/video equipment, lighting, upholstery, carpet and all other exterior and interior components. Boston Carriage Com Inc. has sole discretion over what counts as unusual use or wear.
  • Boston Carriage is not responsible for personal property left in the vehicle.

Meeting your driver

Massport designates a limo stand at every Logan terminal. Step-by-step directions for each terminal and South Station are in our Logan pickup guide.

No-show fee

If the passenger does not show up at the designated location, a no-show fee equal to the total trip cost plus applicable waiting time is charged. To avoid this, please don't leave the pickup location without calling us at 617-314-6538.
