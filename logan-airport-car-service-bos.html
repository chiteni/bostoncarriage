How it works

From touchdown to your front door.

Logan requires every pre-arranged car service to use the designated limo pickup area at each terminal. Unlike rideshare, a dedicated chauffeur is assigned to your trip, and we adjust for your flight in real time.

  1. 01

    We track your flight

    Real-time monitoring means pickup time moves with early arrivals and delays — no extra calls from you.

  2. 02

    Permit from the staging lot

    Your chauffeur checks in at Logan's limo staging lot and heads to your terminal as you land.

  3. 03

    A text when we're curbside

    You get a message the moment your car reaches the limo pickup area at your terminal.

  4. 04

    Bags in, and you're off

    We load the luggage. You sit back for a quiet, private ride home, to the hotel, or the office.

Where to meet your driver

Find the limo stand at your terminal.

Massport designates a limo stand at every terminal. After you collect your bags, follow the directions for your terminal below. Your chauffeur will text you on arrival.

Term.A

Terminal A

Go outside the baggage claim, cross two traffic lanes, and walk to the left to the designated limo stand.

Term.B

Terminal B

Pickups are on the arrival level. Go outside, cross two traffic lanes into the garage and look for the large limo sign. Your driver will be waiting with your name on a sign.

Term.C

Terminal C

From baggage claim, take the elevator to Central Parking, ground level. The limo pickup area is right outside the exit.

Term.E

Terminal E (international)

Proceed to the terminal exit. Go outside, cross two traffic lanes and walk to the left. Your driver will be waiting at the limo stand.

SSt

Boston South Station (all trains)

Exit the South Station terminal onto Atlantic Avenue and walk to the left, toward the taxi stand.

Questions

Logan FAQ.

Do you provide Logan airport car service 24/7?

Yes. Our Logan airport transportation runs 24 hours a day, including holidays and early-morning departures. An early-morning surcharge applies before 6:00 a.m. and after midnight.

Do you track flights for airport pickups?

Yes. We monitor every flight and adjust your pickup time automatically for delays or early arrivals.

How will I know my car has arrived?

Your chauffeur gets a permit from Logan's staging lot, drives to your terminal, and texts you on arrival at the limo pickup area.

Can I book last-minute airport transportation?

Yes, subject to availability. Calling 617-314-6538 is the fastest way.

Is the ride private?

Always. Every ride is private and never shared.
